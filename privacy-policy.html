1. Scope and developer

This policy applies to the Android application PDF Edit Pro - Edit your PDF, package com.ayon.pdfeditpro, developed by Ayon Mallick (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It does not control Android, Google Play, your storage or email provider, a sharing destination, or other third-party apps and services.

Effective date: 17 September 2026
Last updated: 17 September 2026

2. Documents and local app data

You choose PDFs and images with Android’s system file picker. The app can access the items and save destinations you select. Editing and PDF processing occur locally on your device.

  • Selected files: read locally to display and edit them.
  • Working copies: temporarily stored in private app storage while editing.
  • Recent documents: a document name and Android content reference may be stored locally so you can reopen it.
  • Saved files: written to your chosen destination and not uploaded to us.
  • Preferences: app settings may be stored locally.

The app does not use document text, images, signatures, annotations, filenames, or form contents for advertising, profiling, analytics, or model training.

4. Saving, sharing, and support

When you choose Save, Save As, Export, or Share, Android sends the output only to the location or recipient you choose. That provider or app handles the data under its own privacy policy.

If you contact support, your email provider sends us your address, message, attachments, and other information you include. We use it to respond, investigate problems, prevent abuse, and keep necessary business records. Do not attach a confidential PDF unless needed and you are authorized to share it.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Working and share copies remain in private storage or cache until replaced, cleared by Android, removed by clearing app storage, or deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • Documents saved outside the app remain until you delete them from the selected provider.
  • Recent-document entries and preferences can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling.
  • Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to answer requests, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

The app has no user accounts, so there is no server-side app account to delete.

6. Security and permissions

The app uses Android’s Storage Access Framework instead of broad storage permissions. Private files are isolated by Android’s app sandbox, backups are disabled for working data, shared files use scoped Android URI access, and cleartext network traffic is disabled.

No method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device updated, use a screen lock, and share confidential documents only with trusted recipients.

7. Conditional disclosures for an AdMob-enabled release

Not active in the current release. This section applies only if a later version includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Before release, the Play Console “Contains ads” declaration and Data safety form must also be updated.

Data processed by Google and advertising partners

When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and authorized partners may automatically collect or receive:

  • IP address, which may estimate general location;
  • device and account identifiers, potentially including Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other identifiers available to the SDK;
  • app and ad interactions such as launches, views, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information such as launch time, hangs, crashes, and energy usage;
  • consent and privacy-choice signals; and
  • basic device, app, network, and ad-request information.

They may use this data to provide contextual or personalized ads where permitted, measure advertising, limit repeated ads, detect fraud and abuse, maintain security, and improve services. Google and participating partners handle data under their own notices. We will not intentionally provide PDF contents, signatures, annotations, form entries, or imported images to AdMob.

Consent and regional choices

Before requesting ads, an ad-enabled release will use Google’s User Messaging Platform or another Google-certified consent management platform where required. Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland will receive required disclosures and choices before eligible personalized advertising or related device storage is used. A visible privacy-options entry point will be available whenever required.

Eligible residents of applicable U.S. states will receive any required notice and choice to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information as defined by applicable law. Restricted or non-personalized ads may still use limited data for contextual ads, frequency control, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information, or withdraw consent. Because the current app has no account or developer database, most app data can be managed on your device by deleting documents, clearing app storage, revoking document access, or uninstalling.

For support information held by us, email the address below. We may verify requests and retain information where legally required. If advertising is enabled, use the in-app privacy-options control and Google’s advertising controls. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

9. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the current app. Contact us if you believe a child sent personal information in a support request.

If a future ad-supported release is offered to age groups that include children, we will configure age-appropriate treatment, use only permitted SDKs and practices, disable personalized advertising where required, and comply with Google Play Families Policy and applicable law before release.

10. International processing

The current app processes documents locally. Email providers may process support communications in other countries. If AdMob is enabled, Google and advertising partners may process advertising data outside your country under their stated safeguards and applicable transfer mechanisms.

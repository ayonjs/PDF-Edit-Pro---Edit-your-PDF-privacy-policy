Privacy Policy

PDF Edit Pro - Edit your PDF · Effective 16 September 2026

Plain-language summary: PDF editing happens on your device. This version has no account, analytics, advertising SDK, or developer server, and it does not collect or sell personal data.

Files and document content

You choose PDFs and images through Android’s system picker. The app reads only the items you select and stores a temporary working copy in its private app storage while you edit. Documents are not uploaded by the app. Temporary working and sharing copies can be removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.

Data collection and sharing

The current version does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, document content, diagnostics, identifiers, or usage data. When you deliberately use Android’s Share feature or email support, the receiving app and service process what you choose to send under their own privacy policies.

Permissions and security

The app uses Android’s Storage Access Framework and does not request broad storage access. It does not request the Advertising ID, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or notification permissions. App backups are disabled to reduce the chance of document working copies leaving the device through cloud backup.

Children

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Future advertising

This version does not contain Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or any advertising. If advertising is added later, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before release. Where required, the app will ask for consent before ads or related storage are used. Ad technology may then process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices as described in Google’s policies. Users will be given applicable privacy choices. No such processing occurs in the current version.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice.
